Model assumptions

  • Two electron spins, with one isotropically coupled ¹H nucleus.
  • Selectable singlet or triplet initial state, with exponential kinetic model.
  • Scalar exchange coupling J and dipolar coupling D (both in mT), using RadicalPy conventions.
  • Fixed applied magnetic field, with time on the horizontal axis.
  • The rate applies uniform exponential survival exp(−kt). It does not alter coherent spin mixing or represent spin-selective recombination.
  • Singlet + T+ + T0 + T− = 1 for the unrecombined ensemble; total triplet is the sum of the three substates.
  • This is a demonstration model, not a fit to experimental data.
